1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Goa SolarTrack (“we”, “our”, or “the App”), developed and operated by Prashil Wadkar (“Developer”). Goa SolarTrack is a specialized Android utility designed to help rooftop solar prosumers in Goa track their solar power generation, verify solar net-metered electricity bills from the Goa Electricity Department (GED), compute banked energy reserves in compliance with Joint Electricity Regulatory Commission (JERC) regulations, and receive solar panel maintenance reminders.

We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. This document outlines what information is collected, how it is used, where it is stored, and the rights you possess regarding your personal data.

2. Information We Collect and Process

Goa SolarTrack is designed with a local-first architecture, meaning your data remains on your physical device and is only synchronized to secure cloud infrastructure if you choose to enable cloud backup. We collect and process the following categories of data:

Data Type Specific Details Purpose & Usage
Consumer Identifier 11-digit Electricity Consumer Number (e.g. 600xxxxxxxx) Uniquely separates and links your rooftop solar plant profile, ledger entries, and monthly billing calculations.
Solar System Profile System capacity (kW), installation date, panel count, inverter specifications, location, tariff category Used to accurately calculate solar generation potential, monetary savings, and payback analytics.
Monthly Electricity Bill Records Meter reading dates, solar units generated (kWh), units exported, units imported, net billing amounts, and banked energy balances Calculates your monthly electricity balance, verifies net-metering accuracy, and models savings.
Electricity Bill PDF Documents Official electricity bill PDFs selected by the user via the system document picker Processed 100% locally on your device using a local in-memory PDF parsing engine to extract billing numbers. Selected PDF files are never uploaded or transmitted to any third-party server.
Maintenance & Care Logs Dates and records of solar panel cleanings and inspections Provides reminders to maintain peak solar panel efficiency.
Important: We do NOT collect sensitive personal information such as government national IDs, passwords, bank account details, or payment card numbers.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

Goa SolarTrack requests only the minimal device permissions necessary to perform its functions:

  • File & Document Access (Scoped Document Picker): Used solely when you voluntarily tap to upload an electricity bill PDF. We use the standard Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework) to read the selected file in memory. The app does not access your personal photo gallery or unrelated device storage.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver optional local notifications for meter reading due dates, bill receipt reminders, and panel cleaning schedules. You can toggle reminders on or off at any time in the app’s Settings.
  • Network Access: Required to establish cloud synchronization with Google Firebase Firestore and check for application updates.

4. Third-Party Services & Cloud Infrastructure

The app utilizes trusted, industry-standard third-party service providers solely to maintain reliable infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore — Google LLC): Used to store encrypted user records for cross-device synchronization and data restoration. Data stored in Firestore is associated only with your consumer identifier.

Zero Third-Party Advertising: Goa SolarTrack does not integrate any advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Unity, or AppLovin), does not run analytics trackers, and does not track users across other apps or websites.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

Strict No-Sale Policy We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal or solar data to third-party data brokers, marketers, advertisers, or energy aggregators under any circumstances.

6. Data Security

We implement standard, modern security measures to safeguard your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and cloud services are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • On-Device Isolation: Data stored locally on your Android device resides within private, sandboxed app storage, inaccessible to other third-party apps without root access.
  • Cloud Rules: Firestore database security rules enforce boundaries so records are partitioned by consumer identity.

7. User Data Retention & Deletion Instructions

In strict compliance with Google Play’s User Data policy regarding account and data deletion:

  1. Local Data Deletion: You can completely erase all data stored on your device at any time by going to your Android device’s Settings → Apps → Goa SolarTrack → Storage → Clear Data, or by tapping Switch Account / Clear within the app.
  2. Cloud Data Deletion Request: If you wish to permanently delete all records associated with your electricity consumer number from our Google Firebase cloud database, simply email developer Prashil Wadkar at prashiln79@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request - Goa SolarTrack” and include your Consumer Number. Your cloud data will be permanently purged within 7 business days, and confirmation will be sent to you.

8. Children’s Privacy

Goa SolarTrack is a solar and utility utility intended for adults and rooftop property owners. It is not targeted towards or designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.
